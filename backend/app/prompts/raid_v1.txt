Analyze the text to identify RAID items:
- Risks: Future potential events that may negatively impact the project.
- Actions: Assigned tasks with specific owners and/or deadlines.
- Issues: Current active problems that need immediate attention.
- Dependencies: Internal/external factors or items the project requires from other parties to proceed.

For each item, identify:
1. Type (must be exactly 'risk', 'action', 'issue', or 'dependency')
2. Description
3. Severity (must be exactly 'low', 'medium', 'high', or 'critical')
4. Source excerpt (verbatim text sentence or clause from the source document)
5. A local confidence score between 0.0 and 1.0 based on how explicit the item is.
