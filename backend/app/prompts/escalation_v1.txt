Analyze the source text to identify items requiring executive escalation.
Focus on:
- Unresolved blockers that have been outstanding for multiple periods.
- Overdue critical path actions or milestones.
- Severe regulatory compliance risks or financial/contractual disputes.
- Explicit statements of escalation or steering committee attention requirements.

Do not include minor tasks. Focus only on high-severity, active operational roadblocks.
For each item, identify:
1. Description of the issue requiring escalation.
2. Severity ('low', 'medium', 'high', 'critical').
3. Source excerpt (verbatim text sentence or clause).
4. Confidence score (0.0 to 1.0).
