WELCOME TO GLOBALTECH
---------------------
Welcome to GlobalTech Corporation! We are delighted to have you join our team. This handbook is designed to provide you with information about our company, policies, and expectations. Please read it carefully and keep it for future reference.

OUR MISSION
-----------
To innovate technology solutions that empower businesses worldwide while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, excellence, and social responsibility.

OUR VALUES
----------
- Innovation: We embrace creativity and forward-thinking
- Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency
- Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do
- Collaboration: We work together to achieve common goals
- Respect: We value diversity and treat everyone with dignity

EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
------------------

Equal Opportunity Employment
GlobalTech Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

Employment Classification
- Full-time: Regular employees scheduled to work 40 hours per week
- Part-time: Employees scheduled to work less than 40 hours per week
- Temporary: Employees hired for a specific period or project
- Contract: Independent contractors providing services to the company

Probationary Period
New full-time employees serve a 90-day probationary period. During this time, performance and fit are evaluated.

WORK SCHEDULES
-------------

Standard Hours
Our standard business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Full-time employees are expected to work 40 hours per week.

Flexible Work Arrangements
GlobalTech offers flexible work arrangements including:
- Flexible start and end times
- Remote work options (subject to manager approval)
- Compressed workweeks (subject to business needs)

Overtime
Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager. Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a week.

BENEFITS
--------

Health Insurance
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Effective date: First of the month following 30 days of employment
- Company contribution: 80% of premium

Retirement Plan
- 401(k) plan with company match
- Company match: 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary
- Vesting schedule: 3 years

Paid Time Off
- Vacation: 15 days per year (accrues monthly)
- Sick leave: 10 days per year
- Personal days: 3 days per year
- Holidays: 10 paid holidays per year

Other Benefits
- Life insurance
- Disability insurance
- Employee assistance program
- Tuition reimbursement
- Employee discount programs

CODE OF CONDUCT
--------------

Professional Conduct
Employees are expected to:
- Maintain professional behavior at all times
- Treat colleagues and customers with respect
- Protect company confidential information
- Use company resources responsibly

Conflicts of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests. Any potential conflict must be disclosed to HR.

Anti-Harassment Policy
GlobalTech has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind. All employees are entitled to a work environment free from harassment.

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
---------------------

Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted annually. Managers provide ongoing feedback throughout the year.

Performance Improvement Plan
If performance does not meet expectations, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be implemented to provide clear goals and support.

COMPENSATION
------------

Pay Periods
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays.

Pay Increases
Salary reviews are conducted annually based on performance and market conditions.

Bonuses
Performance bonuses may be awarded based on individual and company performance.

SAFETY AND SECURITY
------------------

Workplace Safety
Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe work environment and reporting safety concerns immediately.

Emergency Procedures
Emergency evacuation routes and procedures are posted throughout the building. Regular fire drills are conducted.

Security
All employees must wear identification badges while on company premises. Visitors must be escorted at all times.

COMMUNICATION
-------------

Company Communications
Important company announcements are distributed via email, intranet, and team meetings.

Open Door Policy
Employees are encouraged to discuss concerns with their manager, HR, or any member of the leadership team.

LEAVING THE COMPANY
------------------

Resignation
Employees must provide at least two weeks' notice when resigning. Manager approval is required for shorter notice periods.

Return of Company Property
Upon separation, employees must return all company property including laptops, badges, keys, and documents.
