TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
==========================

Policy Number: FIN-003
Policy Title: Travel and Expense Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Review Date: January 1, 2027
Owner: Finance Department

PURPOSE
-------
This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and expense reimbursement at GlobalTech Corporation to ensure reasonable costs, compliance with regulations, and consistent treatment of employees.

SCOPE
-----
This policy applies to all employees and contractors who incur business travel expenses on behalf of GlobalTech Corporation.

TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION
--------------------

Pre-Approval Requirements
All business travel requires pre-approval from:
- Manager: For travel under $5,000
- Department Head: For travel $5,000 - $25,000
- Vice President: For travel over $25,000

Approval Timeline
- Submit travel requests at least 2 weeks in advance
- International travel requires 4 weeks advance notice
- Last-minute travel requires executive approval

Travel Request Process
1. Complete travel request in expense system
2. Provide business justification and estimated costs
3. Obtain required approvals
4. Receive approval before booking

AIR TRAVEL
----------

Booking Guidelines
- Book through approved travel agency or corporate booking tool
- Economy class for flights under 4 hours
- Premium economy for flights 4-8 hours
- Business class for flights over 8 hours or international travel
- Advance booking: At least 14 days for domestic, 21 days for international

Airline Selection
- Use preferred airlines when available
- Consider total cost including baggage fees
- Direct flights preferred for time efficiency
- Flight changes must be approved by manager

Seat Selection
- Standard seats included in fare
- Premium seat upgrades not reimbursable without business justification
- Emergency exit seats permitted if qualified

GROUND TRANSPORTATION
---------------------

Rental Cars
- Rent compact or mid-size vehicles unless justified
- Use preferred rental companies
- Fuel costs reimbursed with receipts
- Insurance through corporate account, not personal insurance

Ride-Sharing Services
- Uber/Lyft permitted for business travel
- Use business profile when available
- Receipts required for reimbursement
- Pool/Shared rides preferred when appropriate

Taxi Services
- Use taxis when ride-sharing unavailable
- Receipts required
- Tip included in reimbursement (15-20%)

Public Transportation
- Public transit encouraged when cost-effective
- No receipts required for amounts under $25
- Monthly passes reimbursable for regular business travel

ACCOMMODATIONS
--------------

Hotel Guidelines
- Book mid-range business hotels (3-4 star)
- Maximum nightly rate: $200 domestic, $250 international
- Extended stays (5+ nights) consider corporate apartments
- Book through preferred hotel program

Room Type
- Standard rooms unless business justification
- Suites not reimbursable without approval
- Single occupancy standard

Amenities
- Basic Wi-Fi included in room rate
- Parking fees reimbursed with receipt
- Room service not reimbursable without approval
- Minibar charges not reimbursable

MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
-----------------------

Meal Allowances
- Breakfast: $15 maximum
- Lunch: $25 maximum
- Dinner: $40 maximum
- Total daily limit: $75
- Higher limits for high-cost cities with manager approval

Meal Receipts
- Receipts required for meals over $25
- Itemized receipts preferred
- Alcohol limited to 2 drinks per meal
- Gratuities included in reimbursement

Business Entertainment
- Must have clear business purpose
- Pre-approval required for expenses over $200
- Maximum per person: $75 for dinner, $50 for lunch
- Entertainment venues require executive approval

EXPENSE REPORTING
-----------------

Submission Timeline
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of travel
- Late submissions may not be reimbursed
- Outstanding expenses must be submitted before additional travel approved

Required Documentation
- Original receipts for expenses over $25
- Itemized receipts for meals and entertainment
- Travel itinerary and boarding passes
- Business justification for unusual expenses

Expense Categories
- Airfare and transportation
- Lodging
- Meals and incidentals
- Business entertainment
- Conference and event fees
- Visa and passport fees

INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL
-------------------

Additional Approvals
- International travel requires VP approval
- Security assessment for high-risk destinations
- Medical clearance for extended stays
- Embassy registration required

Additional Reimbursements
- Visa and passport fees
- Travel insurance
- Required vaccinations
- Per diem for meals (no receipts required)
- Emergency medical expenses

Currency and Exchange
- Use corporate credit cards when possible
- Exchange rate at time of expense
- Receipts in local currency acceptable
- ATM fees reimbursable

CREDIT CARDS
------------

Corporate Credit Cards
- Corporate cards issued to frequent travelers
- Use for all business travel expenses
- Personal charges not permitted
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately

Personal Credit Cards
- Personal card use permitted when corporate card unavailable
- Reimbursement within standard timeline
- Foreign transaction fees reimbursable
- Interest charges not reimbursable

MISCELLANEOUS
-------------

Business Gifts
- Maximum value: $50 per recipient
- Must comply with anti-corruption policies
- Not permitted for government officials
- Receipts and business justification required

Conference Attendance
- Pre-approval required for conference attendance
- Registration fees reimbursable
- Travel expenses follow standard policy
- Maximum 2 conferences per year per employee

Home Office Expenses
- Not reimbursable for regular employees
- Remote employees may have separate policy
- Equipment provided by company
- Internet stipend for remote workers

COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT
---------------------

Policy Compliance
- All expenses must comply with this policy
- Exceptions require written approval
- Violations may result in disciplinary action
- Random audits conducted regularly

Regulatory Compliance
- Comply with all applicable tax laws
- Follow anti-corruption and bribery laws
- Maintain proper documentation for audits
- Report any compliance concerns

Expense Audits
- Finance department conducts random audits
- Employees must cooperate with audit requests
- Documentation must be retained for 7 years
- Fraudulent expenses result in termination
